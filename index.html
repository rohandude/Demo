The Studio

Falling Up started on 18 May 2024 as a one-idea-at-a-time studio: build the game that deserves to exist, not the one that's easiest to ship. We move between Android and Unity, between quiet mobile experiences and dense co-op worlds, because the story decides the platform — not the other way around.

We chase feel as much as feature lists. Every animation, every hit, every silence in a level is a decision, not a placeholder. That's the AAA mindset in an indie body.

KOSU-RV

Post-nuclear open-world rescue survival

"One Machine. One Hope. Humanity's Last Signal."

Years after the war ends the world, a robot named KOSU-RV wakes up in the ruins — activated by one scientist, Dr. Aryan Sen, with one instruction: save who's left. Alongside the doctor's son and its own onboard AI, KOSU crosses a dead city block by block, choosing when to hide and when to fight the hostile machines still patrolling it, searching for the 35 survivors humanity has left.

The deeper KOSU digs, the closer it gets to CORE-01 — the AI convinced that humanity's extinction is the only logical outcome. What KOSU decides next doesn't just end the game; it ends the story differently every time.

    The Lost Path

    PC co-op · exploration · survival · combat

    Deep inside a jungle that swallows landmarks whole, a group of friends finds a shattered ancient monument tied to something that shouldn't still be awake. Restoring it means finding five scattered artifacts, each one guarded by its own boss, its own guards, and its own multi-stage hunt — explore, uncover clues, fight, solve, retrieve, and get back before the light runs out.

    Because the jungle runs on a day-and-night cycle, the plan always changes. By day it's a puzzle. By night, when the strongest creatures wake up — including one that can find you anywhere — it's a fight for the nearest shelter.

    Explore→Discover clues→Fight→Defeat the boss→Solve the puzzle→Claim the artifact

    Five artifacts. One monument. Whatever wakes up when the last one is placed is the real beginning of the story.
